Can your Mac see the bootloader?

This page only requests USB access, identifies the CH552, and reads its bootloader version and chip UID. It has no erase, program, verify-write, or reset action.

Do not flash anything yet. We have only one tested keypad. A successful probe checks WebUSB access, not that another PCB revision is safe for replacement firmware.
01 / PREPARE

Use Chrome or Edge on macOS

Unplug the keypad. Bridge the empty R12 pads, connect USB-C, then release the bridge. Do not short MCU pins directly or bridge pads while powered.

The USB bootloader should appear as 4348:55E0. If it disappears before you select it, reconnect in bootloader mode and try again.
